FAQ

Questions, answered.

How do I order a design?

Open a design, select Order Now, complete your details and press Place Order. WhatsApp will open with your order information ready to send.

How do I send the payment slip?

After placing the order, complete the bank payment and attach your payment slip in the same WhatsApp conversation.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp contact button and describe what you need. PixelyGraphics can discuss the requirements and quote.
